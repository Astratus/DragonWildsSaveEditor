## Withering Heights

### Seals and switches (the key question)

The premise that the seals are `BP_Seal_Switchable` is **wrong**.

- The three seals are `BP_NPC_Seal_NecromanticFocus` actors.
  - The world save holds three of them, in cells X4_Y-5, X17_Y-4 and X-27_Y-36, and each stores only `bCanBeDamaged`.
  - Each one starts the conversation `Seals.<X>`. Breaking a seal is gated only by `DeathQuest_Beat==4` and `<X>SealBroken` false. Its visuals read the local player's quest state.
- `BP_Seal_Switchable` is the single **Imaru lair door**: participant `ImaruLairTeleportDoor`, holding the CanSpawnImaru and PendingSealDoor tags.
  - It sits in L_World with SwitchPosition 0 in both worlds.
  - It is only touched at Beat 5.
- **So re-breaking the seals after a revert works, and no switch reset is needed.** Nothing about this makes a checkpoint non-offerable.

There is one real world dependency, the **Emberwood village barrier** in front of the Emberwood seal.
- It is opened by four god-shrine switches.
- **World A:** `BP_ArmadylStatue_Switchable`, `BP_GuthixStatue_Switchable_2`, `BP_ZamorakStatue_Switchable` and `BP_SaradominStatue_MissingPiece_Switchable` are all at **1**, so the barrier stays open.
- **World B:** the Fellhollow cells were never saved, so the shrines are at their defaults. The puzzle is replayable in game:
  - The flows are not gated on the WH beat.
  - The Armadyl verdict needs only the ghost-dialogue bools, which are re-learnable.
  - The Saradomin piece dispenser is at 0.
- We touch no switches.

The cleansing pool's progression row is `ActorsInteractedWith BP_CleansingPool_Vines_C`. Neither the live save nor GuestChar persists that actor, so nothing needs removing.

- **WH locations:** every WH key gets its exact flow value or `__absent__`. All `DR_MQ_*` and `DR_GateKeeper` are absent, and BKF is reset to not-started.

## Black Knight's Fortress

### Flow facts

- **Cathan / RegionEntry** (Beat 0): QuestBeat SET 1, objective _1, OldMan=true.
- **WOM:** on the first talk with SpokenToDoric false, it sets objective _2 and DR_MQ_Doric=true. Every first talk sets OldMan=false and SpokenToOldMan.
- **Doric** (Beat 0/1, first talk): objective _3, Doric=false, DreamingStone=true, OldMan=false.
- **The reference player went Cathan, then Doric, then the WOM.** So objective _2 never existed for them, and bkf_speak_doric is rated high: it was built purely from the flow writes.
- **The live DR_MQ_Doric=true is genuine, not a leftover.** Fragments CheckAmount at QuestBeat 0/1 sets QuestBeat -1, objective _4_-1, OldMan=false and Doric=true. That fired when the reference player picked up Fragment_3 before visiting the stone.

### Side content

- The NPC shards need, respectively, 5 Maple Sap, the Glass Owl and the Unusual Trout. All of these are re-obtainable, and they have no quest progression rows.
- The library books and barrier are stateless.

Steps 2 to 4 also remove held shards, the Glass Owl, the Unusual Trout and Broken Titan's Wrath through `inventory_remove`. None of these is currently held.

### Step 6 resolution: ABSENT

- The only writers of GateOpen and FixedDreamstone set them **true**: the repair, plus the catch-up branches in Gatekeeper and UmS SkipDR. Nothing ever writes them false.
- The live save appends both as its last two bools, which confirms they were first written at the repair.
- Every reader tests them with no TestValue (false) or with true.
- An absent bool reads as false. This is shown empirically by GuestChar's NotFoundStones=2, which came from a TestQuestBool-false node while InvestigatedStandingStones was absent.

So the bools are **absent**, and false would behave the same. Adding 13 shards (required, not optional) removes the last inference, and the record is otherwise unchanged. **Upgrade to very high.**
